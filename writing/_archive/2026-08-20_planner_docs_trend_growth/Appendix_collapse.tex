\section{Collapse: derivations and proofs}\label{sec:app:collapse}

This appendix proves the results of Section~\ref{sec:sp:longrun:collapse}: the scope
distinction between damped and catastrophic collapse (\ref{sec:app:collapse:scope}), the
common necessary conditions, Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:collapse} (\ref{sec:app:collapse:lemma}),
the Cobb--Douglas lead case, Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:wh:collapse}
(\ref{sec:app:collapse:cd}), and the two structural results that shape the selection map's
collapse cells: hard essentiality forces finite-time shutdown
(\ref{sec:app:collapse:floor}), and growth capacity precludes damped collapse
(\ref{sec:app:collapse:growthcell}). Notation as in Appendix~\ref{sec:app:longrun},
including its stationary technology ($g_A=g_B=0$ in~\eqref{eq:sp:setup:tfp});
throughout, $g_C<0$ denotes the asymptotic rate of consumption on a collapse path and
$\left|g_C\right|=-g_C$.

\subsection{Damped and catastrophic collapse}\label{sec:app:collapse:scope}

With $\eta>1$, $u\left(C\right)=C^{1-\eta}/\left(1-\eta\right)\to-\infty$ as $C\to0$. Along
$C\sim\bar Ce^{g_Ct}$,
\begin{align}
u\left(C_t\right)e^{-\rho t}\sim-\frac{\bar C^{1-\eta}}{\eta-1}\,
e^{\left[\left(\eta-1\right)\left|g_C\right|-\rho\right]t},
\label{eq:app:col:value}
\end{align}
so lifetime utility is finite if and only if
\begin{align}
\left(\eta-1\right)\left|g_C\right|<\rho .
\label{eq:app:col:damping}
\end{align}
We call a collapse path \emph{damped} when~\eqref{eq:app:col:damping} holds and
\emph{catastrophic} when $U_0=-\infty$. The same quantity governs everything else that must
converge on a collapse path. Since $\lambda\approx u'\left(C\right)\left(1+\zeta\Omega^C\right)^{-1}
\sim e^{\eta\left|g_C\right|t}$ (up to the convergent wedge),
$r\equiv\rho-\dot\lambda/\lambda\to\rho-\eta\left|g_C\right|$, and for any price whose
dividend dies at the common rate $g_C$, the forward integral behind it converges exactly when
$r-g_C=\rho-\left(\eta-1\right)\left|g_C\right|>0$, which is~\eqref{eq:app:col:damping}
again; likewise every transversality product of the form $e^{-\rho t}\lambda\,pZ$ with $p$
decaying at $g_C$ and $Z$ bounded, or $p$ bounded and $Z$ decaying at $g_C$, dies at the rate
$\rho-\left(\eta-1\right)\left|g_C\right|$. \emph{Finite value, convergent price integrals and
transversality are one condition on a collapse path.}

\smalltitle{Scope.} If some feasible path is damped, no catastrophic path is optimal, and the
optimum (if regular) is a damped collapse; this is the case the analysis characterises. If
\emph{every} feasible path is catastrophic --- which
\ref{sec:app:collapse:floor} shows is the generic situation under hard essentiality --- the
discounted criterion assigns $-\infty$ to all of them and loses its selection power;
overtaking-type criteria are not developed here (gap~(xiv) of~\ref{sec:app:longrun:gaps}).

\subsection{Proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:collapse}}\label{sec:app:collapse:lemma}

\emph{(i) The depreciation floor.} The domain restriction $G=I+\delta K\ge0$ of
Problem~\ref{prob:sp} gives $\dot K\ge-\delta K$ pointwise, so $K_t\ge K_0e^{-\delta t}$ and
any exponential rate of $K$ satisfies $g_K\ge-\delta$. Equality is the corner $G=0$: zero
gross investment, the capital stock run down at exactly its depreciation rate, and the goods
constraint then reads $C=Y-C^N-C^D-c^WW$ (consumption is net output; nothing is replaced).

\emph{(ii) The damped price system.} The consumption condition~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:C} with
$\zeta\Omega^C$ convergent to a limit above $-1$ gives $\lambda\sim e^{\eta\left|g_C\right|t}$
and hence $r\to\rho+\eta g_C<\rho$ as in~\ref{sec:app:collapse:scope}. With $q$ convergent
(all normalised prices converge on a regular path, and $q$ is bounded away from zero at any
interior investment margin), the capital costate~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:K} forces its stationary
version in the limit,
\begin{align}
F_K\,w_Y\;\to\;\left(\rho+\eta g_C+\delta\right)\bar q .
\label{eq:app:col:FK}
\end{align}
Positivity of the right side follows from (i) and damping jointly: $g_C\ge-\delta$ gives
$\rho+\eta g_C+\delta\ge\rho-\left(\eta-1\right)\delta$, and
$\left|g_C\right|<\rho/\left(\eta-1\right)$ gives
$\rho+\eta g_C+\delta>\delta-\rho/\left(\eta-1\right)$; whichever bound binds, the two
cannot be negative together, and on the interior of the speed window the limit is strictly
positive.\footnote{If $g_C<-\delta$ (consumption dying faster than capital, a mismatched-rate
profile), only the damping bound is available; the profile is not excluded here and is left
open in gap~(xiii).} Convergence of the remaining forward integrals and the transversality
checks are the computation of~\ref{sec:app:collapse:scope}.

\emph{(iii) The economy dies whole.} The claim is for the collapse cells: regime (E) with
asymptotically essential materials (either branch), or the bounded branch generally.

\emph{Step 1: no material growth without virgin inflow.} Suppose, for contradiction, a
regular path with $K\to\infty$ at $g_K>0$, $R$ of the order of $K$ (so $\Omega\to\bar\Omega>0$),
and $N/R\to0$ --- the loop as its own source. The stream form~\eqref{eq:sp:ledger-streams}
and $R=N+a\varpi W\le N+W$ give
\begin{align}
R\;\le\;N+\bar\Omega\mathcal D+\delta M^K+\mathcal N,
\qquad
M^K\sim\frac{\bar\Omega\phi^IG}{g_K+\delta} ,
\label{eq:app:col:massbalance}
\end{align}
the second from~\eqref{eq:sp:setup:Phi-motion} along the exponential path. Dividing by
$\mathcal D+\phi^IG$ and using $\Omega=R/\left(\mathcal D+\phi^IG\right)\to\bar\Omega$:
\begin{align}
\bar\Omega\;\le\;o\!\left(1\right)+\bar\Omega\left[1-\frac{\phi^IG}{\mathcal D+\phi^IG}\cdot
\frac{g_K}{g_K+\delta}\right],
\label{eq:app:col:massbalance2}
\end{align}
so $\bar\Omega\cdot\frac{\phi^IG}{\mathcal D+\phi^IG}\cdot\frac{g_K}{g_K+\delta}\le o(1)$:
with $g_K>0$ and $\phi^I>0$ this forces $\bar\Omega=0$, a contradiction. Economically:
recycling is a return flow, not a source --- the loop gives back at most what investment
stored, discounted by depreciation and the loop losses, so a growing material side must be
fed from outside. (With $\phi^I=0$ the same conclusion is immediate: $M^K\equiv M^K_0e^{-\delta t}$
and the inflow $N\to0$ cannot cover $R$ of the order of $K$.)

\emph{Step 2: $K$ is bounded.} On the bounded branch, unbounded $K$ with bounded $R$ is
excluded by Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:common}(i) ($r\to A_\infty-\delta$ applies to any regular
path, and consumption cannot then vanish faster than the Euler rate; for kernels with
$A_\infty=0$ the maintenance bill argument of~\ref{sec:app:longrun:feas} applies directly),
and unbounded $K$ with $R$ of its order by Step 1, since regime (E) forces $N\to0$
(Step 2 of~\ref{sec:app:longrun:classification}). On the growth branch the collapse cells
carry essential materials; \ref{sec:app:collapse:growthcell} below shows $K\to\infty$ is
inconsistent with $C\to0$ there.

\emph{Step 3: everything vanishes.} With $K$ bounded and $N\to0$, $R$ bounded away from zero
would require $a\varpi\to1$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:common}(iii), hence $K^R\to\infty$ ---
impossible with $K$ bounded. So $R\to0$; essentiality gives $Y\to0$; if $K\to\bar K>0$ then
$\dot K=Y-C-\delta K-\text{bills}\to-\delta\bar K\ne0$, contradicting convergence, so
$K\to0$; $M^K\le\Phi^KK\to0$ with $\Phi^K$ bounded (an investment-weighted average of the
convergent $\Phi^I$); $-\dot M^K\le\delta M^K\to0$, so the
ledger~\eqref{eq:sp:setup:ledger} gives $W\to0$; and $N\le R\to0$, $D\to0$ (convergent with
finite integral under (E)).

\emph{(iv) The environment recovers and is priced away.} $\Xi\le\left(1+d^W\right)W\to0$, and
with $\theta\!\left(P\right)P$ increasing near zero and $\theta\!\left(0\right)>0$, $P\to0$.
The marginal damage in goods units is
$\mathcal V=v'\!\left(P\right)/\lambda-F_Pw_Y$: the first term dies because $v'\!\left(P\right)$
is bounded near zero while $\lambda\to\infty$ --- \emph{no} condition on $v'\!\left(0\right)$
or on damage exponents is needed, in contrast to both growth configurations --- and the
second converges to $-F_P$ evaluated at the dying limit, which is zero for the workhorse
($F_P=-\Gamma'\mathcal F$ with $\mathcal F\to0$). Hence $p^P\to\bar{\mathcal V}/\left(\bar r
+\theta_0\right)$, equal to zero for the workhorse.

\emph{(v) Scarcity dies in value.} The rent's dividend
$\left|C^N_S\right|w_N\approx\left|C^N_{NS}\right|Nw_N$ dies at the rate of $N$, so
by~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:S} $p^S\sim\left|C^N_S\right|w_N/\left(r-g_N\right)\to0$ under damping.
Once exploration is at its corner the dividend of $X$ is zero, and the transversality
condition (with $X$ bounded and $e^{-\rho t}\lambda$ dying at rate
$\rho-\eta\left|g_C\right|$, of either sign) selects $p^X=0$: worthless deposits behind a
dead margin. \hfill$\qed$

\subsection{The Cobb--Douglas lead case: proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:wh:collapse}}
\label{sec:app:collapse:cd}

Setting: the workhorse at $\varsigma=1$,
$F=\left[1-\Gamma\!\left(P\right)\right]b_0\big(K^Y\big)^{1-\beta}R^{\beta}$ with
$\underline R=0$, $\delta>0$, regime (E) ($\kappa_{\mathrm E}$), and a regular damped collapse
with exponential rates. By Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:collapse}(iii) all quantities die and all
normalised prices converge; the congestion term $c_\mu N^\mu\to0$ is inert. We look for the
\emph{self-similar} profile, $g_C=g_K\equiv g$; mismatched-rate profiles are not excluded
(gap~(xiii)).

\smalltitle{Balanced decay.} By~\eqref{eq:app:col:FK}, $F_K$ converges to a positive
constant. For Cobb--Douglas $F_K=\left(1-\beta\right)Y/K^Y$ and
$F_K\propto\left(R/K^Y\right)^\beta$ (with $\Gamma\to0$ by
Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:collapse}(iv)), so $R/K^Y$ converges to a positive constant: $g_R=g_K$.
Then $Y\propto K^Y\left(R/K^Y\right)^\beta$ gives $g_Y=g_K$, and $F_R=\beta Y/R$ converges:
\emph{the entire material price block is bounded on the way down}, an SS-like price system on
a dying path. From $M^K\sim\bar\Omega\phi^IK$ (the intensity ratio converges),
$g_{M^K}=g$; from the ledger, $g_W=g$; and $N$, when interior, inherits $g$ from the ledger
ratios below.

\smalltitle{The ratio system.} Write hatted ratios per unit of capital,
$\hat Z\equiv Z/K$, and let $k_R\equiv K^R/K$, $s_N\equiv N/R$. The asymptotic system
collects (all objects at their limits):
\begin{align}
&\text{Euler + capital:}
&
\eta g+\rho+\delta&=F_K\bar w_Y/\bar q,
&
F_K&=\left(1-\beta\right)\hat Y/\left(1-k_R\right),
\label{eq:app:col:euler}
\\
&\text{goods:}
&
g&=\hat Y-\hat C-\delta-c_N\bar S^{-\chi}\hat N-c^W\hat W,
\label{eq:app:col:goods}
\\
&\text{materials:}
&
\Psi&=F_R\bar w_Y+\zeta,
&
F_R&=\beta\hat Y/\hat R,
\label{eq:app:col:psi}
\\
&\text{extraction:}
&
\Psi&=c_N\bar S^{-\chi}\bar w_N+\bar p^W\!\left(1+\Omega^{N,S}\right),
\label{eq:app:col:Nmargin}
\\
&\text{ledger:}
&
\hat W\left(1-\bar a\bar\varpi\right)&=\left(1+\Omega^{N,S}\right)s_N\hat R
+\left|g\right|\bar\Omega\phi^I,
&
\hat R&=s_N\hat R+\bar a\bar\varpi\hat W,
\label{eq:app:col:ledger}
\end{align}
together with the waste, treatment and recycling-capital conditions
\eqref{eq:sp:sum:W}--\eqref{eq:sp:sum:KR} at their limits (with $p^P\to0$), the price block
$\zeta=\bar\sigma\left(\bar p^W-\bar p^M\right)$,
$\bar p^M=\delta\bar p^W\!/\left(\bar r+\delta\right)$,
$\bar q=1-\bar\Omega\phi^I\left(1-\bar\sigma\right)\left(\bar p^W-\bar p^M\right)$ with
$\bar r=\rho+\eta g$, the intensity condition defining $\bar\Omega$, the storage share
$\bar\sigma=\bar\Omega\phi^I\left(g+\delta\right)/\hat R$, and $\bar x=k_R/\left(\bar\varpi\hat W\right)$.
Here $g+\delta>0$ is the interior of the speed window
(Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:collapse}(i)); the second term of~\eqref{eq:app:col:ledger} is the
\emph{release from the dying capital stock}, $-\dot M^K=\left|g\right|M^K>0$.

\smalltitle{Count and the role of $\bar S$.} The system determines the ratios, the prices and
the rate $g$ \emph{given} the stranded reserve $\bar S$, which enters only through the
per-tonne cost $c_N\bar S^{-\chi}$ in~\eqref{eq:app:col:goods}
and~\eqref{eq:app:col:Nmargin}. $\bar S$ itself is tied to history: $S$ is monotone once
exploration stops, $\bar S=S_{T}-\int_T^\infty N\,dt$, so the pair (overall scale of the
dying variables, $\bar S$) plays the role initial conditions play on the sustained paths, and
the transition selects the member of the family. As on the BCP and MEG we take the closed
count as evidence of existence without proving it; the count also requires the level
feasibility $\hat Y\ge\hat C+\text{bills}/K$ --- output on the way down must still cover the
mandatory collection bill of the leaking stock, $c^c\hat W>0$.

\smalltitle{Comparative statics of the speed.} In the wedge-light benchmark ($\phi^I$ small,
so $\zeta,\ \bar q-1,\ \bar w_j-1\approx0$), the chain is monotone: a larger $\bar S$ lowers
the per-tonne cost $c_N\bar S^{-\chi}$, hence lowers $\Psi$
by~\eqref{eq:app:col:Nmargin}, hence lowers $F_R$ and raises $\hat R$
by~\eqref{eq:app:col:psi}, hence raises $\hat Y$ and $F_K$, hence raises $g$
by~\eqref{eq:app:col:euler}: \emph{the more affordable the ore the dying economy still holds,
the slower it dies}. The same chain read at fixed $\bar S$ shows the recycling parameters
acting through $\hat R$: cheaper recycling (larger $\psi/x_a$, lower $c_T$) supports a larger
material input at given $\Psi$ and likewise slows the descent.

\smalltitle{The corner variant (run-down collapse).} If the interior
system~\eqref{eq:app:col:euler}--\eqref{eq:app:col:ledger} returns $g<-\delta$, the domain
$G\ge0$ binds instead: $G=0$, $g=-\delta$, $I=-\delta K$, $C=Y-C^N-c^WW$, and
$\sigma=\Phi^IG/R=0$ so $\zeta=0$ and all wedges vanish. The capital costate still requires
$F_K\bar w_Y=\left(\bar r+\delta\right)\bar q$ with $\bar r=\rho-\eta\delta$, so the corner
variant exists only if
\begin{align}
\left(\eta-1\right)\delta<\rho ,
\label{eq:app:col:cornercond}
\end{align}
which is also exactly the damping condition~\eqref{eq:app:col:damping} at $g=-\delta$: the
fastest feasible collapse is damped precisely when it can satisfy the capital margin. When
$\left(\eta-1\right)\delta\ge\rho$ and the interior system has no root inside the damped
window $\left|g\right|<\rho/\left(\eta-1\right)$ --- a poor terminal margin, $F_K$ too small
--- no damped regular collapse exists at all and the path is catastrophic
(\ref{sec:app:collapse:scope}; gap~(xiv)).

\smalltitle{Stranding.} $\int^\infty N\,dt<\infty$ along any exponential $N$, so
$\bar S>0$ unless levels conspire: exhaustion never completes. Two sub-variants, exactly as
for the recycling corners of the stationary state:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \emph{Extractive descent} ($N$ interior forever): the
    margin~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:N} holds along the path and converges
    to~\eqref{eq:app:col:Nmargin} with equality; $s_N\in\left(0,1\right)$; the rent dies with
    the economy, $p^S\sim\left|C^N_S\right|\bar w_N/\left(\bar r-g\right)\sim e^{gt}$, with
    $\bar r-g=\rho-\left(\eta-1\right)\left|g\right|>0$ under damping --- Hotelling on the way
    down.
    \item \emph{Closed-loop descent} ($N=0$ from a finite date): the corner
    inequality~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:N-corner} holds at the frozen $\bar S$ (the remaining ore too
    expensive for the shrinking economy), $s_N=0$, and the
    ledger~\eqref{eq:app:col:ledger} reduces to
    \begin{align}
    \frac{1-\bar a\bar\varpi}{\bar a\bar\varpi}\,\hat R=\left|g\right|\bar\Omega\phi^I :
    \label{eq:app:col:closedloop}
    \end{align}
    the loop's leak exactly equals the release from the dying capital stock --- the economy
    lives on its own carcass, with the circularity gap pinned by the decay rate. Here
    $p^S=0$ (zero dividend behind a dead margin, as for $p^X$ in
    Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:collapse}(v)): the stranded stock is worthless.
\end{itemize}
Which sub-variant obtains, and the date of the switch, are level questions tied to $\bar S$
and the transition (gap~(xiii)).

\smalltitle{Exploration.} $p^S\to0$ and $p^X\to0$ against
$C^D_D\left(0,X\right)w_D=\kappa_{\mathrm E}\!\left(X\right)\bar w_D$ bounded away from zero
(for $X$ bounded away from $\bar X_{\max}$): the corner
condition~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:D-corner} holds strictly from a finite date, $D=0$ thereafter, and
$X\to\bar X<\bar X_{\max}$: the economy stops prospecting before the map is full, and the
undiscovered endowment is stranded along with the reserve.\footnote{Under $\Omega^{D,S}>0$
and $\bar p^W<0$ the credit term works the other way; the overburden artefact of
Remark~\ref{rem:sp:lr:overburden} is bounded here (all prices converge) and cannot reverse
the corner for small $\Omega^{D,S}$.}

\smalltitle{Pollution.} $\Xi=\left[1-\bar\varpi+d^W\bar\varpi\left(1-\bar a\right)\right]W
\sim e^{gt}$, so $P\to0$ at rate $\min\left(\left|g\right|,\theta_0\right)$ and $p^P\to0$ by
Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:collapse}(iv); the treatment margin settles at the interior
SS-like level $\bar\alpha\bar V=c^{T\prime}\!\left(\bar\varpi\right)\bar w_W$ and recycling
at $a'\!\left(\bar x\right)\bar V=F_K\bar w_Y$ when active
(corners as in Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:sss}(iii)--(iv)). No damage-rate condition
appears anywhere: collapse is the one configuration class that never interrogates the damage
tails. \hfill$\qed$

\smalltitle{The classic benchmark.} At $\delta=0$ the lead case degenerates in an
instructive way: Cobb--Douglas is then \emph{not} asymptotically essential
(\ref{sec:app:workhorse:essentiality}), $K\to\bar K>0$ is affordable (no maintenance),
$F_K=\left(1-\beta\right)Y/K\to0$, so $r\to0$ and $g_C=-\rho/\eta$ --- the classic
declining-consumption optimum of the exhaustible-resource literature, recovered as the
$\delta\to0$ boundary of the collapse family. Positive depreciation changes the endgame
qualitatively: balanced decay ($K$ dies too), the depreciation floor $g\ge-\delta$, and a
descent priced off the stranded margin $c_N\bar S^{-\chi}$ rather than off exhaustion.

\subsection{Hard essentiality: finite-time shutdown}\label{sec:app:collapse:floor}

Call essentiality \emph{hard} if there is $\underline R>0$ with $F\left(K,R,P\right)=0$ for
$R<\underline R$ (the workhorse floor), and \emph{soft} if $F>0$ at every $R>0$ while no
feasible path sustains output with $R\to0$ (the workhorse at $\varsigma\le1$,
$\underline R=0$, $\delta>0$).

\begin{lemma}[Shutdown under hard essentiality]\label{lem:app:col:shutdown}
Under hard essentiality, every collapse path is catastrophic: production halts at a finite
date, $C=0$ thereafter, and $U_0=-\infty$.
\end{lemma}

\emph{Proof.} On a collapse path $K\to0$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:collapse}(iii); on the growth
branch via~\ref{sec:app:collapse:growthcell}). Sustaining $R\ge\underline R$ requires
throughput $W\ge R-N\ge\underline R-N$ through the loop with yield $a\!\left(x\right)\varpi<1$
at bounded $x=K^R/\left(\varpi W\right)$, financed by inflows that all die: $N\to0$ under (E)
(or by $R\to0$'s revival logic failing at levels on the bounded branch), and
$-\dot M^K\le\delta M^K\to0$. So the ledger~\eqref{eq:sp:setup:ledger-collected} fails at
$W\ge\underline R$ from some finite date: $R$ crosses the floor, $Y=0$, and the goods
constraint forces $C=0$ (output can no longer cover any consumption; the remaining stocks pay
only the mandatory collection bill while they last). With $\eta>1$,
$u\left(0\right)=-\infty$ on a set of positive measure: $U_0=-\infty$.
\hfill$\qed$

Damped collapse therefore requires soft essentiality; the floor buys the closed DC exit and
the anchored BCP at the price of a catastrophic endgame in every cell whose exit is collapse.
This asymmetry is recorded in the selection maps of
Sections~\ref{sec:sp:longrun:selection} and~\ref{sec:sp:longrun:workhorse}.

\subsection{Growth capacity precludes damped collapse}
\label{sec:app:collapse:growthcell}

Throughout this subsection $\varsigma>1$ and $b_K^{\varsigma/\left(\varsigma-1\right)}-\delta>\rho$
(the growth threshold passed), with regime (E).

\smalltitle{The kernel bound.} From $\mathcal F\ge b_K^{1/\nu}K^Y$ at every
$\left(K^Y,\tilde R\right)$,
\begin{align}
F_K=\left[1-\Gamma\right]b_K\left(\mathcal F/K^Y\right)^{1/\varsigma}
\;\ge\;\left[1-\Gamma\!\left(P\right)\right]A_\infty
\qquad\text{at every point of the domain},
\label{eq:app:col:kernelbound}
\end{align}
not only in the limit: the CES technology above unit elasticity never lets the marginal
product of capital fall below its $AK$ backbone. On any regular collapse $W\to0$, $P\to0$
(Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:collapse}(iv)), so $\Gamma\to0$ and the bound tends to
$A_\infty>\rho+\delta$.

\begin{lemma}[No damped collapse past the growth threshold]\label{lem:app:col:nogrowthcollapse}
In the growth-(E) cells, under $\phi^I=0$ (immaterial capital) no regular collapse path
exists. Under $\phi^I>0$ a regular collapse requires the material wedges to choke the return:
\begin{align}
\frac{\bar w_Y}{\bar q}
=\frac{1-\bar\sigma\bar\pi\,\omega^Y}{1-\phi^I\left(1-\bar\sigma\right)\bar\pi}
\;<\;\frac{\rho+\delta}{A_\infty}\;<\;1,
\qquad
\bar\pi\equiv\left(\bar p^W-\bar p^M\right)\bar\Omega>0,
\label{eq:app:col:wedgechoke}
\end{align}
a level condition on bounded prices whose scale is capped by the collection and treatment
cost parameters through the waste condition~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:W}.
\end{lemma}

\emph{Proof.} With $\phi^I=0$: $\zeta\equiv0$, $q\equiv1$, all wedges are unity, and the
capital costate gives $r=F_K-\delta\ge\left[1-\Gamma\right]A_\infty-\delta\to A_\infty-\delta>\rho$
by~\eqref{eq:app:col:kernelbound}; the Euler equation then forces $\dot C/C\to$ a positive
constant, contradicting $C\to0$. With $\phi^I>0$ the same computation runs through
$r=F_K\bar w_Y/\bar q-\delta$, and $r<\rho$ requires the displayed inequality; the waste
price is bounded on a collapse (all quantities die and $p^P\to0$, so~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:W}
bounds $\bar p^W$ by cost-scale objects), so the condition is a genuine parameter
restriction, not a free divergence. \hfill$\qed$

\smalltitle{The growth-(E)-essential cell is empty of damped regular paths.} Combine the
pieces: sustained consumption in the cell requires the BCP
(Theorem~\ref{thm:sp:lr:classification}), hence the matching conditions of
Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:bcp}; when they fail, no regular sustained path exists. Collapse
in the cell: essentiality past the growth threshold requires the floor
(hard essentiality --- the workhorse's only route to essential materials with
$A_\infty>0$), so by Lemma~\ref{lem:app:col:shutdown} every collapse path is catastrophic.
Hence \emph{no damped regular path of any kind exists in the cell}: the (E)-column twin of
gap~(xii), recorded as gap~(xiv). What the optimum does --- non-regular descent, or a ranking
of catastrophic paths by an overtaking criterion --- is open. The wedge-choked collapse
of Lemma~\ref{lem:app:col:nogrowthcollapse} is the one candidate this argument does not
close for inessential materials ($\underline R=0$), where it would compete with the DC exit;
whether it is ever selected is likewise open.
