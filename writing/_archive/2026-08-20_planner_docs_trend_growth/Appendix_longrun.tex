\section{The long run: derivations and proofs}\label{sec:app:longrun}

This appendix proves the results of Section~\ref{sec:sp:longrun}: Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:common}
(\ref{sec:app:longrun:common}), the classification theorem,
Theorem~\ref{thm:sp:lr:classification} (\ref{sec:app:longrun:classification}), the stationary
state, Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:sss} (\ref{sec:app:longrun:sss}), the balanced circular
path, Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:bcp}
(\ref{sec:app:longrun:feas}--\ref{sec:app:longrun:pollution}), materially expanding growth,
Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:meg} (\ref{sec:app:longrun:meg}), and dematerialised consumption,
Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:dc} (\ref{sec:app:longrun:dc}). Remaining gaps are collected
in~\ref{sec:app:longrun:gaps}. Technology is stationary ($g_A=g_B=0$
in~\eqref{eq:sp:setup:tfp}, so $A=B=1$) everywhere except~\ref{sec:app:longrun:trend}, which
derives the trending system --- Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:branches},
Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:affluence} and Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:trend} --- at the rate
level.

Throughout we use the growth notation $g_Z$ of~\eqref{eq:sp:lr:gZ} and the exponential
benchmark $N_t\sim\bar Ne^{g_Nt}$ with $g_N<0$, so that under exhaustion with $D$ of smaller
order $S_t\sim N_t/\left|g_N\right|$, and the wedge shorthand $w_j\equiv1\pm\zeta\Omega^j$ with
the sign as it appears in the relevant condition.

\subsection{Proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:common}}\label{sec:app:longrun:common}

\emph{(i).} With $q\to1$ (shown in~\ref{sec:app:longrun:prices}; in a bounded limit $q$ is
constant by~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:I}) the asymptotic return on capital is $F_Kw_Y-\delta$. Since
$F_{KK}<0$, $F_K>A_\infty$ at every finite $K$ for the relevant $R$. If $A_\infty-\delta>\rho$,
the return exceeds the discount rate at every finite stock, so no interior stationary $K$
exists --- the stationary version of~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:K} would require
$F_Kw_Y=\left(\rho+\delta\right)q$ --- and $K$ is unbounded. The consumption
rule~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:Cgrowth} with convergent $\zeta\Omega^C$ then delivers
$\eta\dot C/C\to r-\rho$ with $r\to A_\infty-\delta$ for $R$ bounded (on MEG,
$F_K\ge F_K\left(K,\bar R,\cdot\right)$ for any fixed $\bar R$ when $F_{KR}\ge0$, so the
limiting return is no smaller), giving~\eqref{eq:sp:lr:gC}. With
$C,Y,K,G,\mathcal D\sim e^{g_Ct}$ and $R$ bounded,
$\Omega=R/\left(\mathcal D+\phi^IG\right)\sim e^{-g_Ct}$ and
$\sigma\to\bar\sigma\in\left(0,1\right)$. Conversely, if $A_\infty-\delta<\rho$ and $K$ were
unbounded, $r\to A_\infty-\delta<\rho$ makes consumption decline exponentially, contradicting
$\liminf C>0$; so $K$ is bounded, the stationary~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:K} holds at an interior
stock, and $r\to\rho$.

\emph{(ii).} If $\Xi$ does not converge, neither do $P$ and the flow utility. If $C$ is bounded
and $P\to\infty$, flow utility tends to $-\infty$ and the path cannot be optimal; if
$C\to\infty$, the same follows from damage dominance
(Assumption~\ref{ass:sp:lr:regular}(iii)). Stationarity of $P$ requires
$\theta\left(\bar P\right)\bar P=\bar\Xi$; local stability requires $\theta(P)P$ increasing at
$\bar P$; convergence of $p^P$ in~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:P} requires $\rho+\theta+\theta'\bar P>0$.

\emph{(iii).} $R=N+a\varpi W$ with $R$ bounded away from zero and $a\varpi<1$ requires $W$
bounded away from zero. The ledger~\eqref{eq:sp:setup:ledger-collected} is
$W\left(1-a\varpi\right)=N-\dot M^K+\Omega^{N,S}N+\Omega^{D,S}D$; non-negativity of $W$ bounds
$\dot M^K$ by the inflow, and in any limit in which the right side converges, $\dot M^K$
converges to zero (in the stationary state exactly; on the BCP because $M^K$ is bounded,
established in~\ref{sec:app:longrun:feas}). Equation~\eqref{eq:sp:lr:ratelock} follows. If the
inflow vanishes, $a\varpi\to1$; since $a<1$ everywhere and $\varpi\le1$, both $a\to1$ and
$\varpi\to1$, hence $x\to\infty$ and $K^R=x\varpi W\to\infty$. \hfill$\qed$

\subsection{Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:sp:lr:classification}}
\label{sec:app:longrun:classification}

In the regular class every variable converges in the extended sense, so $R$ tends to a point of
$\left[0,\infty\right]$ and the four definitions partition the possibilities, with
$N\le R$ convergent as well. The proof allocates the cells.

\smalltitle{Step 1: the branch.} Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:common}(i): the growth threshold decides
whether the economy is bounded or grows; there is no third case once the knife-edge is set
aside (Assumption~\ref{ass:sp:lr:regular}(i)).

\smalltitle{Step 2: regime (E) kills extraction.} Under (E), cumulative virgin input is finite:
$\int_0^\infty N\,dt=S_0-\lim S+\int_0^\infty D\,dt\le S_0+\bar X_{\max}$. A convergent $N$
with finite integral has $N\to0$. Hence any configuration with permanent extraction --- the SS,
and any MEG path with unbounded virgin inflow --- requires regime (I).

\smalltitle{Step 3: the bounded branch ($A_\infty-\delta<\rho$).} $K,C,Y$ converge to positive
constants (Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:common}(i)).

\emph{No MEG.} With $K$ bounded, saturation (Assumption~\ref{ass:sp:lr:F}(iii)) gives
$Y=F\left(\bar K,R,P\right)=o\left(R\right)$ as $R\to\infty$. Every tonne of $R$ is either
recycled --- passing through $W\ge a\varpi W=R-N$ and paying at least the collection cost
$c^c>0$ --- or virgin, paying extraction and exploration bills that are bounded below per tonne
unless both $C^N_N$ and $C^D_D$ become asymptotically free, a degenerate case we set aside
(\ref{sec:app:longrun:gaps}). Total material costs therefore grow at least linearly in $R$
against output $o\left(R\right)$: the goods constraint~\eqref{eq:sp:goods} fails.

\emph{No BCP (circularity without growth).} If $R$ is bounded away from zero and $N\to0$,
Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:common}(iii) forces $a\varpi\to1$ and $K^R\to\infty$. This is killed twice
over: maintenance of the unbounded $K^R$ is unaffordable in a bounded economy
(\ref{sec:app:longrun:feas}), and the wedge argument of~\ref{sec:app:longrun:prices} breaks the
consumption condition~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:C}.

\emph{The SS cell.} Bounded with $\bar N>0$ requires regime (I) by Step 2. $S$ converges;
$S\to0$ with $\bar N>0$ contradicts reserve depletion
(Assumption~\ref{ass:sp:lr:regular}(ii)): $C^N_N\to\infty$ against a bounded $\Psi$, so the
extraction margin~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:N} could not stay interior. Hence $\bar S>0$, and
$\dot S\to0$ gives $\bar D=\bar N$, with $X$ growing linearly at rate $\bar N$
($g_X=0$, divergent). $\bar a\bar\varpi<1$: otherwise $K^R\to\infty$ as above. $\bar P>0$:
$\bar\Xi=\left[1-\bar\varpi+d^W\bar\varpi\left(1-\bar a\right)\right]\bar W>0$ since
$\bar W>0$ and $\bar a\bar\varpi<1$. Prices converge to the stationary levels of the costate
equations (\ref{sec:app:longrun:sss}). This is the SS structure of the theorem.

\emph{The (E) sub-column.} Bounded with regime (E) forces $N\to0$ (Step 2) and excludes $R$
bounded away from zero (no BCP above), so $R\to0$: collapse under essentiality --- by
Definition~\ref{def:sp:lr:essential} no such path sustains output, hence not consumption ---
and DC without it, with placement proved in~\ref{sec:app:longrun:dc}.

\emph{The (I) sub-column with $R\to0$.} Excluded by viability
(Assumption~\ref{ass:sp:lr:regular}(iv)): a virgin tonne is available at bounded cost (directly
while $S$ is bounded away from zero, through the bounded-cost discovery margin otherwise), the
corner condition~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:N-corner} fails at the candidate limit, and extraction
revives.

\smalltitle{Step 4: the growth branch ($A_\infty-\delta>\rho$).} $K\to\infty$ and $C$ grows at
$g_C$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:common}(i)).

\emph{Under (I), the material side cannot stay bounded.} Suppose $R$ bounded. Marginal
indispensability (Assumption~\ref{ass:sp:lr:F}(i)) gives $F_R\to\infty$, so
$\Psi=F_Rw_Y+\zeta\to\infty$ unless $\zeta$ diverges to $-\infty$, which
\eqref{eq:sp:sum:Omega} ties to $p^W\to-\infty$. The scarcity block stays bounded under (I):
$C^N_N$ is bounded at bounded $\left(N,S\right)$ away from the depletion corner, and $p^S$ is
bounded by the exploration margin~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:D} with $C^D_D$ bounded. Meanwhile the waste
condition~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:W} sends its right side down as $\Psi$ rises, so $p^W$ cannot
diverge to $+\infty$. The extraction condition~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:N} therefore fails towards more
extraction --- and the corner inequality~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:N-corner} fails likewise --- so $N$,
and with it $R$, expands off the candidate path. The same revival argument excludes $R\to0$:
reserves are replenishable at bounded cost forever. Hence growth with regime (I) is MEG.

\emph{Under (E).} $N\to0$ by Step 2, so $R$ bounded away from zero is the BCP. Its remaining
structure is forced as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \emph{No stranded reserves.} Suppose $N\to0$ with $S\to\bar S>0$ and $R$ bounded away
    from zero. Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:common}(iii) forces $a\varpi\to1$, so the price system
    of~\ref{sec:app:longrun:prices} applies and $p^W\to-\infty$. But the scarcity block is
    bounded: $C^N_N\left(0,\bar S\right)<\infty$ and $p^S\to0$ (its dividend is
    $\left|C^N_S\right|\approx\left|C^N_{NS}\right|N\to0$). Then the extraction
    condition~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:N} cannot hold --- its right side tends to $-\infty$ against
    $\Psi\to+\infty$ --- and the corner inequality~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:N-corner} fails for the
    same reason. Extraction revives; optimality selects exact exhaustion. (Transversality does
    not discriminate here: $e^{-\rho t}\lambda p^SS\to0$ on and off the exhaustion path.)
    Exploration only adds to a stock that would be stranded, so maintained or growing reserves
    under vanishing extraction are excluded a fortiori.
    \item \emph{No terminal extraction corner.} With $N=D=0$ from a finite date the ledger
    reads $W\left(1-a\varpi\right)=-\dot M^K$: positive throughput must be financed by running
    down $M^K\ge0$, so the corner is transient unless $a\varpi=1$ at finite $x$, excluded by
    $\lim_{x\to\infty}a=1$. And while circularity deepens, the corner
    inequality~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:N-corner} fails against $p^W\to-\infty$ as above --- its
    right side stays bounded because $S$ is frozen at a \emph{positive} level the moment
    extraction stops: finite-time exhaustion, which would lock the corner behind the
    depletion barrier $C^N_N\left(0,0\right)=\infty$, is excluded by the cumulative clause
    of Assumption~\ref{ass:sp:lr:regular}(ii) (see gap~(xv)). Hence $N>0$
    at every date, dying only asymptotically.
    \item \emph{No terminal exploration corner.} Proved
    in~\ref{sec:app:longrun:exploration}: $D>0$ at every date, dying asymptotically against the
    regime-(E) blow-up, with $X\to\bar X_{\max}$.
\end{itemize}
Two exits remain in the cell: $R\to\infty$ --- unbounded throughput on a finite cumulative
virgin inflow --- is excluded by the level-matching argument of~\ref{sec:app:longrun:matching},
which pins a bounded stationary throughput $\bar W$ (see the caveat
in~\ref{sec:app:longrun:gaps}); and $R\to0$ is DC, placed in~\ref{sec:app:longrun:dc}.
\hfill$\qed$

\subsection{BCP feasibility: Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:bcp}(i)--(ii) and growth necessity}
\label{sec:app:longrun:feas}

\smalltitle{Implied recycling capital.} Inverting the tail~\eqref{eq:sp:lr:tails} against the
rate lock~\eqref{eq:sp:lr:ratelock}: with $1-a\sim c_1N$,
$c_1\equiv\left(1+\Omega^{N,S}\right)/\bar W$, the power tail gives
$x\sim\left(c_1N/\gamma_a\right)^{-1/\psi}\sim e^{\left|g_N\right|t/\psi}$; the exponential
tail gives $x\sim\left|g_N\right|t$ and $K^R$ linear in $t$.

\smalltitle{Part (i): dematerialising capital.} The binding constraint is
$\dot M^K\le\left(1+\Omega^{N,S}\right)N+\Omega^{D,S}D\to0$.

\emph{Case $\delta=0$.} By~\eqref{eq:sp:setup:Phi-motion}, $\dot M^K=\Phi^IG=\sigma R$ with
$\sigma=\phi^IG/\left(\mathcal D+\phi^IG\right)$ under the baseline: for small $G$,
$\sigma R\approx\phi^I\left(R/\mathcal D\right)G$ is of the order of $G$, so the ledger requires
$G\lesssim N$ eventually and $\int^\infty G\,dt<\infty$: the capital stock is bounded,
contradicting $K^R\to\infty$. (Void if $\phi^I=0$, immaterial capital, not our baseline.)

\emph{Case $\Phi^I=\bar\Phi^I$ constant (or exogenous, bounded away from zero).} Then
$\frac{d}{dt}\left(M^K-\bar\Phi^IK\right)=-\delta\left(M^K-\bar\Phi^IK\right)$, so
$M^K\to\bar\Phi^IK$ and $\dot M^K\to\bar\Phi^I\dot K>0$ permanently along any path with growing
capital, violating the constraint for any $\delta$.

\emph{Baseline with $\delta>0$.} $\dot M^K=\sigma R-\delta M^K$ with $\sigma\to\bar\sigma$, so
$M^K\to\bar\sigma\bar R/\delta$ bounded even as $K\to\infty$ (the dilution $\Omega\to0$ at
work), and $\dot M^K\to0$ as required; that the approach respects the inequality at every date
is a transition-path condition we do not characterise.

\smalltitle{Growth necessity (used in the classification proof).} Maintenance forces unbounded
output: $\delta K\ge\delta K^R\to\infty$ inside~\eqref{eq:sp:goods} requires
$\sup_{K^Y}\left[F-\delta K^Y\right]=\infty$, i.e.\ $A_\infty\ge\delta$; willingness to hold
the unbounded stock (Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:common}(i)) sharpens this to $A_\infty-\delta>\rho$.
A second, independent argument runs through the wedges (\ref{sec:app:longrun:prices}).

\smalltitle{Part (ii).} $c^WW\to\left[c^c+c^T(1)\right]\bar W$ must be finite. \hfill$\qed$

\subsection{BCP prices and rate pinning: Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:bcp}(iii)}
\label{sec:app:longrun:prices}

\smalltitle{The waste-margin identity.} At $\varpi=1$ the pollution bracket
in~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:W} is $d^W\left(1-\alpha\right)$, so
\begin{align}
p^W\left(1-\alpha\right)=c^Ww_W+p^Pd^W\left(1-\alpha\right)-\alpha\Psi ,
\label{eq:app:lr:wasteFOC}
\end{align}
and substituting into $V\equiv\Psi-p^W+d^Wp^P$ the $p^P$ terms cancel identically:
\begin{align}
V=\frac{\Psi-c^Ww_W}{1-\alpha}.
\label{eq:app:lr:Videntity}
\end{align}
From $\alpha=a-a'x$: the power tail has $a'=\gamma_a\psi x^{-\psi-1}$,
$1-\alpha=\gamma_a\left(1+\psi\right)x^{-\psi}$,
$\left(1-\alpha\right)/a'=\left(1+\psi\right)x/\psi$; the exponential tail has $a'=1-a=e^{-x}$,
$1-\alpha=\left(1+x\right)e^{-x}$, $\left(1-\alpha\right)/a'=1+x$. Combining
\eqref{eq:app:lr:Videntity} with the capital margin $a'V=F_Kw_Y$
in~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:recycling-virgin-KR}:
\begin{align}
\Psi-c^Ww_W=\frac{1-\alpha}{a'}F_Kw_Y\;\approx\;\frac{\psi+1}{\psi}\,x_t\,F_Kw_Y
\;\longrightarrow\;\infty\ \text{like }x_t .
\label{eq:app:lr:identity}
\end{align}

\smalltitle{The waste price.} From~\eqref{eq:app:lr:wasteFOC},
$p^W=d^Wp^P+\left[c^Ww_W-\alpha\Psi\right]/\left(1-\alpha\right)$; with $\Psi$ of order $x_t$
and $1-\alpha$ of order $N_t$ ($x_tN_t$ for the exponential tail),
\begin{align}
p^W\sim-\frac{\Psi}{1-\alpha}\sim-P_0e^{g_{p^S}t},
\qquad
g_{p^S}=\left|g_N\right|\frac{\psi+1}{\psi}
\quad\left(\text{exponential tail: }g_{p^S}=\left|g_N\right|\right).
\label{eq:app:lr:pW}
\end{align}
$-p^W$ is the marginal circularity multiplier times the per-pass surplus of a tonne of waste.

\smalltitle{$\zeta$ and the wedges.} For $p^W\sim-P_0e^{g_{p^S}s}$, the forward
solution~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:M} gives
\begin{align}
p^M=\frac{\delta}{r+\delta-g_{p^S}}\,p^W\left(1+o(1)\right),
\qquad
p^W-p^M=p^W\frac{r-g_{p^S}}{r+\delta-g_{p^S}}\left(1+o(1)\right),
\label{eq:app:lr:pM}
\end{align}
(convergence needs $r+\delta>g_{p^S}$), so $\zeta=\sigma\left(p^W-p^M\right)$ diverges with
$p^W$ at rate $g_{p^S}$. Every wedge is
$\zeta\Omega^j=\zeta\Omega\omega^j\sim e^{\left(g_{p^S}-g_C\right)t}$: bounded because
$g_C\ge g_{p^S}$ (i.e.\ $e_R\le1$), converging to zero when strict (then $w_j\to1$,
$u'/\lambda\to1$, and $q=1-\Phi^I\left(1-\sigma\right)\left(p^W-p^M\right)\to1$ since
$\Phi^I\left(p^W-p^M\right)\sim e^{\left(g_{p^S}-g_C\right)t}$). In a \emph{bounded} economy
$\Omega$ is bounded away from zero, $\zeta\Omega^C\to-\infty$, and~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:C} fails
--- the second growth-necessity argument used in the classification proof (circularity without
growth).

\smalltitle{Rate pinning.} $\Psi=F_Rw_Y+\zeta$: along the growth path $F_R\sim e^{e_Rg_Ct}$
and $\zeta\sim-Ze^{g_{p^S}t}$, while by~\eqref{eq:app:lr:identity} their sum grows only like
$x_t$. The two leading exponentials must cancel: equal rates,
\begin{align}
e_Rg_C=g_{p^S}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
g_N=-\frac{\psi}{\psi+1}\,e_Rg_C,
\label{eq:app:lr:gpin}
\end{align}
and equal leading coefficients (one level condition among the constants of the path). This
proves part (iii); $r>g_{p^S}$ is required in~\eqref{eq:app:lr:pM} and
in~\ref{sec:app:longrun:matching} below. \hfill$\qed$

\subsection{BCP extraction matching: Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:bcp}(iv)}
\label{sec:app:longrun:matching}

The interior extraction margin~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:N} makes the scarcity block cancel the
diverging waste charge:
\begin{align}
C^N_Nw_N+p^S=-p^W\left(1+\Omega^{N,S}\right)+O\left(x_t\right)
\sim P_0\left(1+\Omega^{N,S}\right)e^{g_{p^S}t}.
\label{eq:app:lr:scarcity}
\end{align}
Along the path
$C^N_N\left(0,S_t\right)\sim\kappa\left(\left|g_N\right|/\bar N\right)^\chi
e^{\chi\left|g_N\right|t}$. The rent moves at the same rate: $C^N\left(0,S\right)=0$ implies
$C^N_S\left(0,S\right)=0$, so
$\left|C^N_S\right|\approx\left|C^N_{NS}\right|N\sim\chi\left|g_N\right|C^N_N$,
and~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:S} makes $p^S$ of order $C^N_N$, finite iff $r>\chi\left|g_N\right|$.
Matching rates in~\eqref{eq:app:lr:scarcity}: $\chi\left|g_N\right|=g_{p^S}$, i.e.\
$\chi=\left(\psi+1\right)/\psi$. Matching levels adds one equation, which together with
exhaustion (pinning $\bar N$) determines the stationary throughput $\bar W$; we take the closed
count of level conditions as evidence of existence without proving it. The same level condition
is what excludes unbounded throughput under regime (E) in the classification proof: the system
has no degree of freedom left with which to let $\bar W$ diverge.

\smalltitle{Mismatches.} If $\chi<\left(\psi+1\right)/\psi$ the block diverges too slowly, the
right side of~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:N} falls below $\Psi$, and extraction expands off the path. If
$\chi>\left(\psi+1\right)/\psi$ it diverges too fast and pushes $N$ to its corner, which cannot
be sustained either (the terminal-corner step of~\ref{sec:app:longrun:classification}).
Hyperbolic profiles $N\sim t^{-\phi}$, $\phi=\chi/\left(\chi-1\right)$, reconcile the second
case only when $g_C=0$, i.e.\ on the knife-edge $A_\infty-\delta=\rho$: any $g_C>0$ makes $F_R$
and $\zeta$ exponential while the hyperbolic path needs $\Psi$ logarithmic. \hfill$\qed$

\subsection{BCP treatment margin (part of Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:bcp})}
\label{sec:app:longrun:treatment}

With $\alpha\to1$ and $V\to\infty$ the gain $\alpha V+p^P\left(1-d^W\right)$ diverges. If
$c^{T\prime}(1)<\infty$ the upper branch~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:varpi-upper} binds at a finite date:
full treatment arrives in finite time. If $c^{T\prime}\sim\left(1-\varpi\right)^{-\nu}$, the
interior branch gives $1-\varpi\sim V^{-1/\nu}$, compatible with the rate
lock~\eqref{eq:sp:lr:ratelock} iff $\nu\le\left(\psi+1\right)/\psi$; since $c^T(1)<\infty$
already requires $\nu<1$, Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:bcp}(ii) is the binding condition.
\hfill$\qed$

\subsection{BCP exploration margin: Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:bcp}(v) and the
exploration-corner step}\label{sec:app:longrun:exploration}

Suppose $D=0$ on $\left[t_D,\infty\right)$ with $\bar X=X\left(t_D\right)$ and
$C^D_D\left(0,\bar X\right)<\infty$. The corner condition~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:D-corner} requires
$p^S+p^X\le C^D_D\left(0,\bar X\right)w_D+p^W\Omega^{D,S}$ for all $t\ge t_D$. But
$p^S\sim e^{g_{p^S}t}\to\infty$ by~\eqref{eq:app:lr:scarcity}, $p^X$ is a discounted forward
integral of the bounded flow $C^D_Xw_D$~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:X} and stays bounded, and the
$p^W\Omega^{D,S}$ term (a credit once $p^W<0$) lowers the right side further. The inequality
fails at a finite date. Hence on the BCP the discovery margin~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:D} holds with
equality asymptotically, $D\to0$ never reached, which requires regime (E) of
Assumption~\ref{ass:sp:lr:discovery} with the blow-up rate-matched to $p^S$; the exact matching
exponent is not derived here. \hfill$\qed$

\subsection{BCP pollution: Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:bcp}(vi)}
\label{sec:app:longrun:pollution}

$p^P$ cancels from~\eqref{eq:app:lr:Videntity} but survives inside
$p^W$~\eqref{eq:app:lr:wasteFOC} and hence in~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:N}. Along the growth path
$\lambda\approx u'(C)\to0$, so $\mathcal V=v'(P)/\lambda-F_Pw_Y$ carries the factor
$1/u'(C)\sim e^{\eta g_Ct}$. If $v'(0)>0$ then $\mathcal V\gtrsim e^{\eta g_Ct}$ with
$\eta g_C\ge g_C>g_{p^S}$ for $\eta\ge1$ and $e_R<1$: $p^P$ outruns the material prices, the
$d^Wp^P$ term flips $p^W$ to $+\infty$, and the right side of~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:N} exceeds
$\Psi$ permanently --- extraction is forced to its corner and the BCP structure collapses. With
$v'(0)=0$ and $v$ smooth, $v'\left(P_t\right)\sim v''(0)P_t$ with $P_t$ decaying at rate
$\min\left(\left|g_N\right|,\theta(0)\right)$ (the emission flow is
$\Xi\sim d^W\left(1-a\right)W\sim N$), giving the stated rate condition, alongside
$\left|F_P\right|$ growing slower than $e^{g_{p^S}t}$. \hfill$\qed$

\subsection{The stationary state: Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:sss}}
\label{sec:app:longrun:sss}

With all controls constant and $\zeta\Omega^C$ constant, \eqref{eq:sp:sum:Cgrowth} gives
$r=\rho$, and the costate equations~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:K}--\eqref{eq:sp:sum:M} collapse to
levels:
\begin{align}
p^M=\frac{\delta p^W}{\rho+\delta},
\quad
p^W-p^M=\frac{\rho\,p^W}{\rho+\delta},
\quad
\zeta=\sigma\frac{\rho\,p^W}{\rho+\delta},
\quad
p^S=\frac{\left|C^N_S\right|w_N}{\rho},
\quad
p^X=-\frac{C^D_Xw_D}{\rho}\;(\to0),
\label{eq:app:lr:ssslevels}
\end{align}
and $p^P=\mathcal V/\left(\rho+\theta+\theta'\bar P\right)$.

\emph{(i).} Combining $q=F_Kw_Y/\left(\rho+\delta\right)$ from the
stationary~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:K} with
$q=1-\Phi^I\left(1-\sigma\right)\left(p^W-p^M\right)$ from~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:I} and the middle
identity in~\eqref{eq:app:lr:ssslevels} yields
$F_Kw_Y=\rho+\delta-\Phi^I\left(1-\sigma\right)\rho\,p^W$.

\emph{(ii).} Substituting the stationary rent and $p^X$ into the exploration
margin~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:D} gives~\eqref{eq:sp:lr:inventory} (with the $p^X$ term vanishing
under regime (I); if $C^D_X\to c>0$ it appears as $+c\,w_D/\rho$ on the right).

\emph{(iii).} At $\varpi=0$ the gain in~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:varpi-lower} is
$\mathcal G^\varpi=\alpha V+p^P\left(1-d^W\right)\ge p^P\left(1-d^W\right)>0$ since $p^P>0$
unconditionally and $\alpha V\ge0$ at any candidate optimum, while
$c^{T\prime}(0)=0$: the lower corner never binds.

\emph{(iv).} Direct from the Kuhn--Tucker system~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:KR}; at $\bar K^R=0$, $x=0$
gives $\alpha=0$ and the treatment margin reduces as stated.

\emph{Closure.} The stationary system has 17 unknowns
($\bar C,\bar N,\bar\varpi,\bar x,\bar W,\bar\Omega,\bar K,\bar S,\bar P,\bar\lambda$;
$p^W,\zeta,p^S,p^X,p^P,p^M,q$) against 17 equations (eight first-order conditions, five
stationary costates, three static constraints, pollution stationarity), with $\bar D=\bar N$,
$I=0$, $\bar M^K=\sigma\bar R/\delta$ built in. Existence additionally requires $\bar C>0$ in
the goods constraint; uniqueness is open (the sign of $p^W$ is not determined a priori, and
regimes of either sign are conceivable). \hfill$\qed$

\subsection{Materially expanding growth: Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:meg}}
\label{sec:app:longrun:meg}

\emph{(i).} First, $W\to\infty$. From $R=N+a\varpi W\le N+W$, $R\to\infty$ requires
$W\to\infty$ or $N\to\infty$; and if $N\to\infty$ with $W$ bounded, the
ledger~\eqref{eq:sp:setup:ledger-collected} makes
$\dot M^K=\left(1+\Omega^{N,S}\right)N+\Omega^{D,S}D-W\left(1-a\varpi\right)\to\infty$, so
$M^K\to\infty$, and the stream form~\eqref{eq:sp:ledger-streams} gives
$W\ge\delta M^K\to\infty$ (here $\delta>0$ is used) --- a contradiction. Next, by
Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:common}(ii),
$\Xi=\left(1-\varpi\right)W+d^W\varpi\left(1-a\right)W$ converges, and both terms are
non-negative, so each is bounded. The first, with $W\to\infty$, gives $\varpi\to1$; with
$\varpi$ thus bounded away from zero and $d^W>0$, the second gives $\left(1-a\right)W$
bounded, hence $a\to1$, with $x\to\infty$ fast enough that
$\left(1-a\left(x\right)\right)W$ stays bounded. Using
$1-a\varpi=\left(1-\varpi\right)+\varpi\left(1-a\right)$, $W\left(1-a\varpi\right)$ is
bounded. (If $d^W=0$ the argument only forces $\left(1-\varpi\right)W$ bounded --- treated
waste emits nothing --- and the recycling margin is disciplined by prices alone.)

\emph{(ii).} Immediate from the ledger~\eqref{eq:sp:setup:ledger-collected} rearranged,
$\dot M^K=\left(1+\Omega^{N,S}\right)N+\Omega^{D,S}D-W\left(1-a\varpi\right)$, with the last
term bounded by (i): an unbounded virgin inflow must be matched by unbounded net storage. On
the growth path $\dot M^K=\sigma R-\delta M^K$, so a constant asymptotic storage share
$\bar\sigma>0$ of the growing $R$ delivers it.

\emph{(iii).} From (i), $a\to1$ requires $x\to\infty$, and with $W\to\infty$ also
$K^R=x\varpi W\to\infty$, so $K\ge K^R\to\infty$. A bounded economy admits no MEG
(\ref{sec:app:longrun:classification}, Step 3), so $A_\infty-\delta>\rho$, and when
$F_{KR}\ge0$ the limiting return is at least $A_\infty-\delta$, giving
$g_C\ge\left(A_\infty-\delta-\rho\right)/\eta$ by the Euler equation with convergent
$\zeta\Omega^C$ --- reading $A_\infty$ at the path's limiting pollution stock and presuming
$w_Y\to1$ (or $\zeta\le0$), both parts of the open price system
(\ref{sec:app:longrun:gaps}). \hfill$\qed$

\medskip
Under the workhorse family the configuration closes entirely --- rates, prices, and
FOC-consistency, with every hedge above resolved --- in Appendix~\ref{sec:app:workhorse:meg}.

\subsection{Dematerialised consumption: Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:dc}}
\label{sec:app:longrun:dc}

\emph{(i).} Immediate from Definition~\ref{def:sp:lr:essential}: sustained consumption
requires $\liminf Y>0$ through the goods constraint, which no feasible dematerialising path
delivers under essentiality.

\emph{(ii).} Suppose $R\to0$ with $S\to\bar S>0$ (dematerialisation before exhaustion). The
deep-circularity price system of~\ref{sec:app:longrun:prices} does not apply --- $W\to0$ and no
rate lock forces $a\varpi\to1$ --- but the conclusion needs only
Assumption~\ref{ass:sp:lr:F}(i): on the growth branch, $K\to\infty$ with $R\to0$ gives
$F_R\to\infty$, hence $\Psi=F_Rw_Y+\zeta\to\infty$ with the remaining prices bounded (given
tame damages), while the right side of~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:N-corner} stays bounded at
$C^N_N\left(0,\bar S\right)<\infty$; on the bounded branch the same failure of the corner is
the viability condition (Assumption~\ref{ass:sp:lr:regular}(iv)). Either way the corner fails,
extraction revives and feeds $R$: material input cannot be abandoned while reserves remain
available at bounded cost. Dematerialisation is therefore possible only after asymptotic
exhaustion, when the recycled tonne's supply cost (the diverging capital margin) is what
$F_R$'s divergence must be weighed against --- the same comparison as the matching condition
in~\ref{sec:app:longrun:matching}. Stranding is excluded as
in~\ref{sec:app:longrun:classification}, so exhaustion is exact, and the exploration margin
dies against the diverging discovery cost as in~\ref{sec:app:longrun:exploration},
$X\to\bar X_{\max}$.

\emph{(iii).} Under regime (I) the argument in (ii) applies at every date: replacement through
discovery stays available at bounded cost, so a virgin tonne is always on offer, the corner
conditions~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:N-corner} and~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:D-corner} cannot jointly hold
against a diverging (growth branch) or viable (bounded branch) material value, and extraction
revives. Hence DC requires regime (E). \hfill$\qed$

\subsection{The trending system}\label{sec:app:longrun:trend}

This subsection derives the trending results of the main text at the rate level: the growth
structure (Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:branches}), the material-price pace, the affluence lemma
(Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:affluence}), and the cell arguments behind
Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:trend}. The workhorse rate systems are
in~\ref{sec:app:workhorse:trend}. Throughout, ``wedges behave'' means $\zeta\Omega^j$
converge, $w_Y\to1$ and $q\to1$; this is maintained as part of regularity on trending paths
and is itself a gap-level assumption (gap~(xvi)).

\smalltitle{Intensive form.} Under Assumption~\ref{ass:sp:lr:F}(iv) write $m=BR$,
$k=K^Y/m$, so $Y=A\,m\,f\left(k\right)$, $F_K=Af'\left(k\right)$,
$F_R=AB\left[f\left(k\right)-kf'\left(k\right)\right]$, with $f$ read at the path's limiting
pollution stock. The capital margin with behaved wedges gives $r\to Af'\left(k\right)-\delta$.

\smalltitle{Explosion (Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:branches}(i)).} $F_{11}<0$ gives
$F_1>A_\infty$ at every finite $K$ for the relevant $m$, so with $A_\infty>0$ and $g_A>0$,
$F_K\ge A\,A_\infty\to\infty$ and $r\to\infty$. The consumption
rule~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:Cgrowth} then forces $\dot C/C\to\infty$: no finite $g_C$ exists, so
neither a sustained-consumption nor a collapse path lies in the regular exponential class.
Two caveats survive: diverging wedges ($w_Y\to0$) or damage saturation
($\Gamma\left(P\right)\to1$ on a path with $P\to\infty$, disciplined but not excluded by
damage dominance) could choke the return at bounded values; both are recorded in gap~(xvii).

\smalltitle{The trend-paced branch (Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:branches}(iii)).} On a regular path
with finite $g_C$, the Euler equation pins $r\to\rho+\eta g_C$, hence
$Af'\left(k\right)\to\rho+\delta+\eta g_C$. Two cases. \emph{(a) $g_A=0$:} $k\to k^*$ with
$f'\left(k^*\right)=\rho+\delta+\eta g_C$, a level condition solvable exactly when the
demanded return lies in the range of $f'$. For a CES kernel above unit elasticity that range
is $\left(A_\infty,\infty\right)$, and solvability is precisely the reversed effective
threshold $A_\infty-\delta<\rho+\eta\left(g_B+g_R\right)$; when it fails, $k\to\infty$ and
the economy is on the endogenous branch (item (ii) of the lemma, which is the stationary
Step 4 verbatim since $A=1$). Below unit elasticity the range is
$\left(0,f'\left(0\right)\right)$ and solvability is a viability condition on the kernel
($f'\left(0\right)>\rho+\delta+\eta g_C$; workhorse: $b_K^{\varsigma/\left(\varsigma-1\right)}$
exceeds the demanded return). With $k$ settled, $g_K=g_m=g_B+g_R$ and
$g_Y=g_m$, so $g_C=g_B+g_R$. \emph{(b) $g_A>0$ (requires $A_\infty=0$):}
$f'\left(k\right)\sim e^{-g_At}$ forces $k\to\infty$ along the tail
$f'\sim\mathrm{const}\cdot k^{-\epsilon_\infty}$, so $g_k=g_A/\epsilon_\infty$ and
$g_K=g_A/\epsilon_\infty+g_m$. Output:
$g_Y=g_A+g_m+\beta_\infty g_k=\gamma_Ag_A+g_m$. The capital-output ratio moves at
$g_K-g_Y=g_A\left(1-\beta_\infty-\epsilon_\infty\right)/\epsilon_\infty\le0$ by the
consistency condition, so the investment share is bounded (vanishing when strict, settling
in the Cobb--Douglas case) and consumption absorbs output: $g_C=g_Y=\gamma_Ag_A+g_B+g_R$,
provided the material bills ($C^N$, $C^D$, $C^W$) are subdominant --- checked configuration
by configuration in~\ref{sec:app:workhorse:trend}. Transversality on capital holds
throughout: the exponent of $e^{-\rho t}\lambda qK$ is
$-\rho-\eta g_C+g_K\le-\rho-\left(\eta-1\right)g_C<0$ for $g_C\ge0$, and on the collapse
side it is exactly the damping condition~\eqref{eq:sp:lr:damping}.

\smalltitle{The material-price pace.} $F_R=AB\left(f-kf'\right)$. On the trend-paced branch
the asymptotic material share $F_RR/Y\to1-\beta_\infty>0$ is bounded away from zero, so
$F_R\sim Y/R$ and $\pi\equiv g_{F_R}=g_C-g_R$; with $R$ settled, $\pi=g_C=\gamma_Ag_A+g_B$.
On the endogenous branch (CES above unit elasticity) the share dies and the direct
computation gives
$\pi=\left(g_C-g_R\right)/\varsigma+\left(1-1/\varsigma\right)g_B$, the
generalisation of $e_Rg_C$; the stationary case is $g_B=0$, $g_R=0$. In every case
$\pi\le g_C-g_R$, with equality on the trend-paced branch --- the fact the affluence lemma
uses.

\smalltitle{Affluence prices pollution (Lemma~\ref{lem:sp:lr:affluence}).} Suppose a regular
sustained path has $g_C>0$ and $P\to\bar P>0$. With behaved wedges $\lambda$ dies at rate
$\eta g_C$, so $\mathcal V\ge v'\left(\bar P\right)/\lambda$ diverges at $\eta g_C$; the
discount in the $p^P$ integral converges to $\rho+\eta g_C+\theta+\theta'\bar P$, which
exceeds the growth of $\mathcal V$ by $\rho+\theta+\theta'\bar P>0$, so $p^P$ diverges at
$\eta g_C$, and via the waste condition~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:W} (whose $p^P$ coefficient is
bounded below by $d^W\left(1-\alpha\right)>0$) so does $p^W$. Every delivered-material value
grows at most at $\pi\le g_C<\eta g_C$; since
$\zeta=\sigma\left(p^W-p^M\right)$ with $\sigma<1$, the net $p^W$ coefficient on the
extraction margin is $\sigma-\left(1+\Omega^{N,S}\right)<0$, so the treatment
gain~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:gains} diverges (corner $\varpi=1$, reachable since
$c^T\left(1\right)<\infty$) while the extraction margin diverges negatively (corner $N=0$)
in finite time; exploration follows (its product is worthless with extraction shut). All
inflows to the loop then die, the ledger runs the loop on the bounded storage release
$\delta M^K$, cumulative emissions are finite, and the sink drains $P\to0$ ---
contradicting $\bar P>0$. The same divergence argument on the congestion side rules out
$\bar P>0$ with $R\to\infty$. Hence $P\to0$ on every regular trending sustained path, and
the rate at which $v'\left(P\right)/\lambda$ and $\left|F_P\right|$ must die transfers the
$\beta_v,\beta_F$ conditions to every cell with the cell's own rates.

\smalltitle{Regime (I): the SS cell empties into MEG
(Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:trend}(i)--(ii)).} Consider a trending (I)-path with $R$
bounded. By the affluence lemma $P\to0$, so the pollution charges are bounded; under the
backstop the virgin supply cost is bounded ($C^N_N$ at bounded $N$ with $S$ a produced
inventory, $p^S$ pinned by replacement cost $\bar\kappa$); but the delivered value grows at
$\pi>0$. The extraction condition~\eqref{eq:sp:sum:N} cannot hold with both sides settling:
the margin is strictly profitable from a finite date, extraction expands, and $R$ bounded
fails. $N\to0$ fails the same way (the revival argument of the stationary Theorem, now with
a diverging left side). Hence $R\to\infty$: MEG, disciplined --- as in the stationary
(I)-growth cell --- only by congestion, with the parametric system
in~\ref{sec:app:workhorse:trend}.

\smalltitle{Regime (E).} Cumulative virgin input is bounded by $S_0+\bar X_{\max}$
regardless of trends, so $N\to0$ in the regular class: the column winds down exactly as at
stationary technology, and the exits (BCP / DC / collapse, floor variants included) are as
in the main text, with the BCP rate system run at the pace $\pi$
(\ref{sec:app:workhorse:trend}) and DC/collapse split by the sign of
$\gamma_Ag_A+g_B+g_R$.

\subsection{Remaining gaps}\label{sec:app:longrun:gaps}

(i) Existence of the BCP is established at the level of rates and the count of level
conditions, not as a fixed-point theorem; existence of the stationary state likewise (the
substantive condition being $\bar C>0$), and uniqueness of neither is established. (ii) The
matching exponent for the discovery-cost blow-up (\ref{sec:app:longrun:exploration}) is not
derived. (iii) Profiles outside the exponential class: subexponential \emph{sustained growth}
is confined to the knife-edge $A_\infty-\delta=\rho$ (Remark~\ref{rem:sp:lr:exponential}), but
slowly varying corrections ($t^\beta e^{gt}$) to the exponential rates are unexplored, and
nothing is said when $F_K$ has no limit (failure of Assumption~\ref{ass:sp:lr:F}(ii)).
(iv) The transition-path condition
$\dot M^K\le\left(1+\Omega^{N,S}\right)N+\Omega^{D,S}D$ on the BCP is assumed, not
characterised. (v) The pollution rate condition should be sharpened once functional forms for
$v$, $\theta$, $F$ are fixed. (vi) Uniqueness and comparative statics of the stationary state,
and the local (saddle-path) dynamics around it, are left to the quantitative part. (vii) MEG is
placed and constrained (Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:meg}) but, in the general family, not
characterised: the joint rates of the material block and the supporting price system are open,
as is FOC-consistency of the configuration itself, and the growth bound of
Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:meg}(iii) borrows from that open price system (it presumes the
material wedges vanish, or $\zeta\le0$, and reads $A_\infty$ at the path's limiting pollution
stock). Under the workhorse family all of this closes
(Appendix~\ref{sec:app:workhorse:meg}, Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:wh:meg}), with existence
again established at the level of rates and the count of level conditions, not as a fixed
point. What remains open in general: a characterisation free of the workhorse's parametric
tails, and, relatedly, the exclusion of unbounded throughput under regime (E), which rests on
the level-matching argument of~\ref{sec:app:longrun:matching} rather than a direct proof. (viii) DC is placed (Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:lr:dc}) but the profile of the phase-out
path --- the decay rates of $R$, $W$, $K^R$ and the associated price asymptotics --- is not
derived. (ix) The degenerate configurations excluded by saturation and viability
(Assumptions~\ref{ass:sp:lr:F}(iii) and~\ref{ass:sp:lr:regular}(iv)) --- asymptotically free
virgin tonnes, economies for which materials are never worth their supply cost --- are set
aside rather than characterised. (x) The regular class itself (extended-sense convergence) is
an assumption: cycles and other non-convergent optimal paths are excluded by scope, not by
proof. (xi) Under a material floor ($\underline R>0$ in the workhorse family,
Appendix~\ref{sec:app:workhorse}), the BCP rate system is pinned only to an interval,
$g_{p^S}\in\left[g_C/\varsigma,\,g_C\right]$; which point the optimum selects --- by level
conditions, transversality, or the transition --- is not derived. (xii) When the workhorse's
MEG conditions fail --- congestion below the threshold $\mu\le\left(\psi+1\right)/\psi$, or
the damage rate conditions of Proposition~\ref{prop:sp:wh:meg} violated --- the growth-(I)
cell admits no regular exponential sustained-consumption limit at all
(Appendix~\ref{sec:app:workhorse:meg}); what the optimum then does --- non-regular paths,
slowly varying rates, or no sustained consumption --- is not derived. Relatedly, the MEG
analysis scopes out exploration overburden ($\Omega^{D,S}=0$): with $\Omega^{D,S}>0$ and a
bounded discovery cost the overburden artefact of Remark~\ref{rem:sp:lr:overburden} is
binding, and only resolving the stock-flow caveat of
Section~\ref{sec:sp:setup:materialaccounting} would discipline it.
(xiii) The collapse analysis (Section~\ref{sec:sp:longrun:collapse},
Appendix~\ref{sec:app:collapse}) characterises the self-similar profile ($g_C=g_K$) only:
mismatched-rate profiles (consumption dying faster than capital) are not excluded; the
selection between the interior and corner speeds, and between the extractive and closed-loop
sub-variants, is a level-and-transition question tied to the stranded reserve $\bar S$; and
existence is again by rate-and-count. (xiv) When no damped regular path exists --- the
growth-(E)-essential cell with failed matching (the (E)-column twin of gap~(xii)), any
collapse cell under hard essentiality, or a damped window
$\left|g_C\right|<\rho/\left(\eta-1\right)$ that the level system cannot reach --- every
path has $U_0=-\infty$ and the discounted criterion cannot rank them; overtaking-type
criteria are not developed. The wedge-choked collapse of
Lemma~\ref{lem:app:col:nogrowthcollapse} (material wedges pushing the return below the
threshold at bounded prices, $\phi^I>0$) is not resolved. (xv) If the cumulative clause of
Assumption~\ref{ass:sp:lr:regular}(ii) fails (workhorse: $\chi<1$), exhaustion can complete
in finite time at finite cost and the extraction corner locks behind the depletion barrier:
an \emph{exhaustion-locked} circular variant --- the loop financed by storage release, deep
circularity without a live extraction margin --- then evades the per-date interiority of
Theorem~\ref{thm:sp:lr:classification}(ii) and, with the floor anchoring throughput, appears
constructible at the level of rates on the growth branch even where the matching conditions
fail; whether it exists as an optimum is not derived. (xvi) The trending system
(\ref{sec:app:longrun:trend}, \ref{sec:app:workhorse:trend}) is established at
rate-and-count only: no level system is closed, existence and uniqueness are not
established in any trending cell, and the wedge dynamics along trending paths --- the
convergence of $\zeta\Omega^j$, $w_Y\to1$, $q\to1$, and the subdominance of the material
bills in the goods constraint --- are maintained as regularity rather than derived.
Relatedly, exhaustiveness of the four configurations under trends leans on extended-sense
regularity of the detrended system, and the trend-regular kernel
(Assumption~\ref{ass:sp:lr:F}(iv)) adds constant returns that the stationary analysis never
needed. (xvii) The explosive cell ($g_A>0$ with $A_\infty>0$) is excluded by scope, not
resolved: super-exponential paths are unclassified, the damage-choked
($\Gamma\to1$) and wedge-choked variants of the diverging return are open, and nothing is
said about what the optimum in that cell does. (xviii) The floor-anchored trending BCP ---
the candidate that rescues the growth-(E)-essential cell and the floor-collapse cells ---
is rate-consistent only: its matching analogues, the level system, and its optimality
against catastrophic alternatives are not derived. (xix) Trending DC and trending collapse
are placed by the rate race $\gamma_Ag_A+g_B+g_R\gtrless0$ but not profiled: $g_R$ itself
is not pinned (the trending twin of gap~(viii)), the collapse profiles of
Appendix~\ref{sec:app:collapse} are stationary-derived with no trending counterpart, and
whether any cell optimally collapses at a positive trend vector is open.
