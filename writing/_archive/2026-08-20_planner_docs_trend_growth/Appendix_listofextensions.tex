\section{Extensions and open items}\label{sec:app:extensions}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \emph{Differential technological progress in the material loop.} The trends
    of~\eqref{eq:sp:setup:tfp} live in goods production only; the recycling yield $a$, the
    extraction, discovery, collection and treatment costs are stationary in final-good units,
    which makes the material-handling side share Hicks-neutral progress one for one. The
    extension is progress at \emph{different} rates --- a rising recycling yield or a falling
    treatment cost relative to goods --- which would tilt the loop's margins over time rather
    than only through prices.
\end{itemize}
